About the company

Twenty-two harvests of building soil health

Incorporated in 2002 and headquartered in Indore, Madhya Pradesh, Agro Phos India Limited runs manufacturing facilities in Dewas and Meghnagar producing Single Super Phosphate, NPK mixtures, Zinc Sulphate, phosphate-rich organic manure (PROM) and calcium-sulphate soil conditioner.

Beyond its own product line, the company trades DAP, Urea and Ammonium Sulphate to round out what dealers and cooperatives need each season, backed by an in-house testing lab that checks every batch against grade.
